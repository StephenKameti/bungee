SensitiveSamiljeol (Mon 1 Mar) is a national day of remembrance for the 1919 independence movement. Post 12 rides the long weekend and never names or sells the day

Dates to confirm before scheduling. Seollal 2027 falls on Saturday 6 February (lunar new year). The holiday block is the day before, the day and the day after: Friday 5 to Sunday 7 February, with a substitute holiday on Monday 8 February because the block touches a Sunday. Confirm the substitute day in the government's published 2027 holiday list. Jeongwol Daeboreum is the 15th day of the first lunar month, Saturday 20 February 2027. Graduation dates vary by school, mostly the first three weeks of February.
