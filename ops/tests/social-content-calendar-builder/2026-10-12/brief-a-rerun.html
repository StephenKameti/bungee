Week 3: 15 to 21 February. Graduation season, then Jeongwol Daeboreum

Context: graduation ceremonies run through the month, with bouquets at the school gate and the traditional jjajangmyeon lunch after. Daeboreum, the first full moon of the lunar year, is Saturday 20: the morning custom is bureom, cracking hard nuts against boils and for strong teeth, alongside five grain rice and dried greens. The nut loaf sells that weekend only.
