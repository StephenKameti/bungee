Audience architecture · Shema Smile · Kigali · Term 2, January 2027

Brush for tomorrow's closeness, not the dentist.

Three tiers for a new toothpaste and a night brush habit, built on a Kimironko compound, a tailoring stall and a Nyamirambo comedy crew.

Tap a ring to open its tier. Centre: the teen who brushes. Middle: the mother who buys. Outside: who gives permission.
01 Tier 1 · Primary · the user Ineza, 15 Senior 3, day school in Remera · Kimironko · Ubudehe C
Who they are
13 to 17. Lives with her mother and two brothers in a rented brick house; father works upcountry. Kinyarwanda at home, English at school.
Day in the life
16:30, the kiosk by the moto stage near Kimironko market. A 25 g tube at eye level she can buy with her own coins.
Key psychographic
She wants a fresh, confident smile, but the night brush feels like a child's rule and the tube is never hers.
Brand relationship
Never heard of it. Barrier: not her decision. She must believe the night brush is what confident people do.
Strategic implication
Short video 21:00 to 22:00, youth radio after homework, the moto stage kiosk. Cast real teens in uniform, never a bathroom.

Nta muntu ushaka kuvugana n'umuntu ufite umwuka mubi.

Nobody wants to talk to someone with bad breath. Native check before use.

Give her a tube that is hers, and a night brush that is about tomorrow.

02 Tier 2 · Secondary · the buyer Mukamana Claudine, 41 Tailor, Kimironko market · Ubudehe C
Who they are
35 to 50, mothers who run the shopping. Three children, a stall, an ikimina of six market women.
Day in the life
16:00 Saturday, after Umuganda and the ikimina. The market toothpaste shelf: the largest tube she trusts at the lowest price.
Key psychographic
She wants the best for their teeth, but the budget rewards the tube that lasts longest.
Brand relationship
Never heard of it. Barrier: trust and habit. A health worker she trusts and a price that matches her usual tube.
Strategic implication
Kinyarwanda morning and evening radio with a health voice; Saturday demos at Kimironko market; ikimina meetings.

Umwana wanjye agomba kumwenyura nta soni.

My child must smile without shame. Native check before use.

Mum buys the trust. Ineza buys the habit.

03 Tier 3 · Amplifier · the permission Gisa, 19 Skit maker, Nyamirambo · and the school health clubs
Who they are
18 to 25 skit makers and the teacher patrons of school health clubs. Street Kinyarwanda, English punchlines.
Day in the life
19:00 Friday, posts a skit about the moment before a close talk. By morning it is in every class group.
Key psychographic
Wants to be the funniest voice in the city; fears being called a sellout.
Brand relationship
Never heard of it. Barrier: being scripted. Needs a brand that can take a joke.
Strategic implication
Seed 10 skit makers in term week one; Gisa visits health clubs with the patron.

Ubuzima ni ugusetsa, ariko ntusetse ufite umwuka mubi.

Life is about laughing, but don't laugh with bad breath. Native check before use.

Make the night brush a joke everyone is in on.

Cross-tier strategic read

Ineza needs the night brush to belong to her, not to her mother. Claudine buys on trust and price; Gisa and the health clubs turn the habit into a shared joke. In Kigali, teens brush for tomorrow's closeness, not for the dentist.

Prices are estimates; confirm with a shelf check. Kinyarwanda lines for a native read before artwork.
