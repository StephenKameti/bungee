Audience architecture · Nuri Table · Seoul · Launch February 2027

Living alone is fine. Eating like nobody cares is not.

Three tiers for the launch of a weekly box of home style side dishes, built on Gwanak officetels, a mother in Daegu and a studio kitchen in Mangwon.

Tap a ring to open its tier. Centre: the buyer. Middle: who shapes her choice. Outside: who gives permission.
01 Tier 1 · Primary · the buyer Seo Jiwon, 31 Payroll accountant · wolse officetel, Sillim · 3rd quintile in a band of 2nd to 4th
Who they are
27 to 36, lives alone in a 23 square metre officetel in Sillim, works at Guro Digital Complex. Women lead; men living alone a strong second.
Day in the life
19:20, Sillim station exit. A KRW 4,900 convenience store lunchbox and a cup of soup. The pickup locker by the door is the brand moment.
Key psychographic
She wants to eat like someone looks after her, but cooking for one wastes food, money and the evening.
Brand relationship
Never heard of it. Barrier: fear of waste. She must believe it is enough for one and none of it ends in the bin.
Strategic implication
Own the walk home: station walls, pickup lockers, short video 22:00 to midnight. Cast her content, never lonely.

편의점 도시락 세 번이면 일주일 끝. 엄마 반찬은 냉동실에서 잠자고.

Three convenience store lunchboxes and the week is over. Mum's side dishes sleep in the freezer.

She is not lonely at dinner. She is tired of eating like nobody cares.

02 Tier 2 · Secondary · the sender Lee Mihwa, 58 Pharmacy counter, part time · Suseong district, Daegu · 3rd to 4th quintile
Who they are
52 to 65, mothers of children living alone in Seoul. Owns an apartment in a 1990s complex. Gyeongsang dialect.
Day in the life
11:30, the courier counter. A 6 kilogram chilled parcel of Seollal leftovers to Sillim. Too much and not enough at once.
Key psychographic
She wants to keep feeding her child from 240 km away, and knows half of every parcel goes uneaten.
Brand relationship
Never heard of it. Barrier: pride and taste. Sending it must still feel like her cooking.
Strategic implication
The messaging app gift shop as media after Seollal and before Chuseok. Frame it as mum's weekday kitchen.

반찬 보내도 반은 버린다는 거, 나도 알아.

I know half the side dishes I send get thrown away.

Let mum send the weekdays. She already sends the holidays.

03 Tier 3 · Amplifier · the permission Kang Doyun, 27 Solo living creator · half basement studio, Mangwon
Who they are
24 to 34 solo living creators. Video editor by day, a cooking channel from one induction ring by night.
Day in the life
18:00, posts a 60 second short. The brand moment: a one week, one fridge shelf challenge with a Nuri Table box.
Key psychographic
Lives on trust; one scripted ad and the comments turn.
Brand relationship
Never heard of it. Barrier: sounding like an ad. Needs freedom to test it badly on camera.
Strategic implication
Seed 15 creators after Seollal, no script. A Mangwon market pop up with Doyun cooking from the box.

혼자 사는 게 문제가 아니라, 혼자 먹는 게 귀찮은 거지.

Living alone isn't the problem. Eating alone is just a hassle.

If Doyun can't break it on camera, it's real.

Cross tier strategic read

Jiwon does not need saving; she needs dinner to stop being a negotiation she loses at the convenience store door. Her mother's parcel is the real competitor and the real ally. In Seoul, living alone is fine; eating like nobody cares is not.
