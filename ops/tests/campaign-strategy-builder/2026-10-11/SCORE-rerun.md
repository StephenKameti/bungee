## 4. Verdict

**Brief A rerun: Yes.** A senior strategist would send this to the board as it stands. The thinking that already passed is intact, and every reason the first run failed is gone: copy follows "Spanish first" because the skill now says to, the budget split, funnel and phasing are the skill's own requirement and add up, the palette is a labelled proposal with the colours to avoid, the claims step catches risky lines before they are written, and there are no brackets or notes to self. The board also gets what it asked for, three insights and a reasoned choice. The only open line is the author name on the title page, which in a live session the skill's single up front question fills; here it sits as Assumption 1, as the skill requires.

**Watch items, not blockers:**
* The city's app workforce (230,000 to 300,000) is an inference from the national pilot total, stated as one; the November qual or Fieldnote's own data should confirm it.
* Every street register line still needs a read aloud by a Mexico City native writer before production, as the skill's own quality check expects.
* "Te guarda" remains a judgement call for compliance; the fallback line is written and ready.
* This rerun was made by the same agent that applied the fixes. Before launch, a blind run on a third market (Asia or Europe) would confirm the gains hold for a runner who has not read the fix list. Brief B was not rerun.
