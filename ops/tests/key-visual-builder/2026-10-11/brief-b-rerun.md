### 01 Monday to Sunday (Safe)

**Why it wins.** It shows the claim as evidence, day by day, readable in three seconds at a stall. (Product truth, Graphic flat)

**Set in.** Umoja, Nairobi, Kenya.

**Layout logic.** A grid of four across and two down sits on flat yellow: seven stages of the bar plus the wrapped pack in the eighth cell. Day labels go under each bar in post. The top 28 percent carries the headline; the bottom 14 percent carries the endline and the price plate.

### 02 Wash Day (Safe)

**The idea.** On Saturday morning a mother stands in front of a line heavy with the family's whole week of washing, and the bar on her basin is still a proper bar.

**Why it wins.** The full line and the still solid bar in one frame is the claim, proved by the person who matters. (Hero portrait, Graphic flat)

**Set in.** Kayole, Nairobi, Kenya. Fire route, so a full Uganda variant is set in Nansana, Kampala.

**Layout logic.** She stands in the right half, waist up, level gaze to camera; the line crosses behind her at shoulder height; basin, bar and wrapped pack sit lower left. The top 26 percent is yellow wall above the line for the headline; the lower right 16 percent is clear ground for the price plate.

## 8. Close

**Fire immediately**

1. **Wash Day.** The human proof: a mother, a full line and a bar that is still a bar. Safest to approve, and the Nansana variant carries it into Uganda.
2. **By Wednesday.** The win back argument for switchers, once the comparison is substantiated. It names the switch without naming anyone. The Kireka variant carries it into Uganda.
3. **Per Day.** Turns the mandatory price into the persuasion: divide by seven and the price argument flips. Built in Kampala, with a Githurai variant for Kenya.

**The disruptive alternate: Siku Saba.** Put it beside Wash Day: the claim built as a giant 7 of laundry. A seven reads in English, Swahili and Luganda, it can be shot for real with a drone in one day, and the same stunt can be laid out at the market entrance on activation day.

**What is missing**

* Bar size and the claim basis (household size, loads per week). Every proof line, and the By Wednesday comparison, stays suspended until the client's wear test is in hand.
* Shelf price in KES and UGX, the recommended retail price legal line, and whether a fixed price can run on billboards for the whole flight. Every figure in this book is a marked placeholder.
* Uganda language: keep the mandated Swahili on Kampala executions, or let Luganda take its place. Client decision.
* A native Luganda writer to check the local line Sabbuuni emu, wiiki yonna (including the word for week) and the word musanvu, and to write the Luganda radio versions for central region stations.
* Pack artwork, the real brand palette and any emboss on the bar. The palette here is proposed; the bar face is generated plain.
* Billboard sites and sizes per market, confirmed with the media owners. The 4:1 plates assume 40 x 10 ft sites.
* Radio: stations per market and spot lengths. Scripts are written at 15 s; 30 s versions on request.
* Activation detail: which markets, stall and canopy sizes, and the demo mechanic, so the roll up, valance and bunting can be sized.
* Social format. Social is listed without video; if status video or short video is in scope, motion briefs get written for the fire routes.
* Decision date.
