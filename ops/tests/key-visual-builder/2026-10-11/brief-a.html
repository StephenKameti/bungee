The brief, read back

So everyone signs off on the same problem

Single minded proposition

The first quiet cup before the city wakes is yours. (Verbatim.)

Product heroAlba single serve ground coffee sachets. Format assumed: a drip bag, a paper filter pouch with fold out arms that hook over the cup rim, each in its own outer sachet, sold in a box of 10. To confirm.
Business problemCategory entry and trial. Urban workers take their first coffee standing at a café counter on the way to work, so ground coffee at home never gets a moment.
Insight (proposed)In Lisbon and Porto the first coffee of the day is drunk standing at a counter, in under a minute, between strangers. It is a habit, not a moment. Nobody has a first cup that is only theirs.
AudienceUrban workers 25 to 40 renting flats in older buildings in Lisbon and Porto, out of the door by 7:45, breakfast skipped. In their words: não tenho tempo, bebo uma bica no caminho. (No time. I grab a bica on the way.)
ChannelsOut of home (bus shelter panels, 8x3 m roadside), social including short vertical video, in store (shelf talker, gondola end).
MandatoriesThe pack on every key visual. Portuguese headline with an English secondary line.
PaletteProposed, see the palette lock: Alba Night #1E2A44, First Light #F3C9A1, Lime White #F4F0E8, Roast #4A2F22, Porto Granite #7D8084.
BudgetMid (stated in the brief; the spine has no budget row, recorded here).
No gosNone stated. Applied: no identifiable café, coffee or capsule brand in frame; no claim the brief has not cleared (origin, strength, blend, sustainability); no postcard Lisbon or Porto.
Market layer

No market module on file for Portugal. Built from the brief and from research into Lisbon and Porto, as SKILL.md instructs.

  • Casting. Urban Lisbon and Porto are mixed cities. Cast by neighbourhood and moment, for example a woman with brown skin and dark curly hair on an enclosed glass balcony in Cedofeita. February work wear is layered: wool, knit, a work shirt underneath. No tourist styling.
  • Places. Lisbon: Arroios, Anjos, Penha de França, Graça, Campo de Ourique. Older buildings, tall sash windows, deep stone sills, lime washed or tiled facades, small kitchens. Porto: Bonfim, Cedofeita, Paranhos, Miragaia. Grey granite, tiled facades, enclosed glass balconies, steep streets. Postcard shorthand stays out: the yellow tram, the river bridges, the viewpoint terraces.
  • Light. February: first light about 7:00 and sunrise about 7:30 in Lisbon, a few minutes later in Porto. That half hour is the quiet window the proposition lives in. Lisbon light bounces off pale facades; Porto mornings often carry Atlantic mist over grey granite.
  • Language. European Portuguese, informal tu for 25 to 40, never Brazilian vocabulary: chávena, never xícara. The word for breakfast carries a spelling hyphen in Portuguese, so the copy is written around it. Local register: Lisbon's café espresso is a bica, Porto's is a cimbalino. English secondary line mandated on every KV.
  • Retail. Modern trade: hypermarkets, supermarkets and the small city centre proximity stores urban workers use on the way home. The coffee aisle is crowded with capsules and ground coffee; shelf talkers and gondola ends carry the launch, plus sampling.
  • Media. Street furniture panels at bus shelters and metro stations (about 1185 x 1750 mm, backlit, many now digital in both cities), 8x3 m roadside panels, short vertical video on the mainstream social platforms.
  • Calendar. Pointer only, confirm the live year: the clocks change on the last Sunday of March and of October, which moves the quiet window by an hour; the return to work in early January and in September.
Open questions and the working assumption used
  • Which single serve format is it: a drip bag that hooks over the cup, or a steeping bag? Assumed: Drip bag.
  • Is there pack artwork and a brand palette? Assumed: Proposed palette and pack colourway, replaced on receipt.
  • Which month does it launch? Assumed: February 2027: first light about 7:00, sunrise about 7:30.
Register held across the setWarm documentary, held in a pale dawn key: lime white walls catching first light, so the set survives backlit shelter panels and the coffee aisle. One register for all seven routes.

The Sill

Your cup is already brewing on the window sill while the café across the street still has its shutter down.

Why it wins. The city's café has not even opened and yours already has, which is the proposition in one look.

Product truth · Warm documentary · No people (a kettle tilting in from the edge) · Pack in scene · Lisbon, Arroios (Porto variant: Bonfim)
