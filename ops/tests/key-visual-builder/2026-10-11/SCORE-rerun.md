## 3. Verdict

**Brief B rerun: Yes.** A senior creative director would send this to the client as the first route presentation. Uganda is now handled the way a Kampala client expects: English leads, the mandated Swahili sits second, and one Luganda line goes to a native writer before artwork, with the Swahili mandate raised as the client's call. Radio has a format, a length and a station language per market, and price runs through every route as a marked placeholder in the right currency. The six routes keep the strong ideas from the first run (The Line, Per Day, Siku Saba), and the three fire routes travel to the other market with full variants. Every open item under What is missing is a client question, not a gap in the work.
