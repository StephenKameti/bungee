**The idea.** The school bell, turned upside down on the teacher's desk, is full to the brim with steaming uji.

**Why it wins.** Uji before the bell, made literal and absurd and dead serious; nobody forgets it, and it is one cheap prop shoot. (Object poetry, Warm documentary)

**Set in.** Changamwe, Mombasa.

**Layout logic.** Bell on the centre line, books left, pouch right. Blackboard above for the headline in chalk white; desk front below for the endline and the price plate.

## 8. Close

**Fire immediately**

1. **Dakika Tano.** The proposition as proof, clock to clock, and the strongest read at a duka and at speed.
2. **Cup of Dawn.** The brand name made into a picture; it can carry Mawio for years.
3. **The Pour.** The safe pick: one cheap food shoot covers every format and both cities.

**The disruptive alternate: Bell Full.** Put it beside The Pour. One is the product, the other is the school morning turned into a trophy, and parents will forward it.

**What is missing**

* The real palette, logo and pouch artwork. The proposed palette is a working palette only.
* Shelf prices in TSh and KSh, and whether a smaller pouch is planned for kiosks.
* Which nutrition claims, if any, are cleared. All are suspended, and Bell Full headline B waits on it.
* Whether Swahili may lead on the Kenyan coast, and a native writer for every Swahili line, one in Dar and one in Mombasa.
* Billboard sites and vehicle back sizes from the media owners, and the sign writer brief for painted walls.
* Ramadan 2027 dates, if the client wants an iftar wave after back to school.
