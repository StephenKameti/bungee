The brief, read back

Single minded proposition

Clouds are not cover. (Verbatim.)

Product heroHalfshade Daily SPF 50+ face fluid, 50 mL soft squeeze tube with a flip cap.
Business problemCategory usage gap: people own sunscreen but use it only at the beach. Win the everyday, grey day, commute occasion.
Insight (proposed)On a grey Melbourne morning nobody thinks about the sun, so nobody reaches for sunscreen. The burn that turns up that evening feels like bad luck. It is not luck. It is the cloud they trusted.
AudienceCity workers 25 to 40, commuting by tram, bike or on foot, who wear sunscreen at the beach and skip it on the way to work. In their words: it's cloudy, I'll be right.
ChannelsTram supersides, CBD street furniture 6 sheets, roadside billboards, social feed and stories, 6 second bumpers, pharmacy shelf talkers.
MandatoriesPack on every KV. SPF 50+ visible. Legal line on every KV: Always read the label. Follow the directions for use.
BudgetMid. Six routes with the full kit; the bumpers for the three fire routes only.
Price and currencyPrice does not show on the KVs. Pharmacy shelf price is set by the retailer; no price plate.
PaletteProposed, none supplied: Halfshade Coral #FF5A3C, UV Violet #5B2BD9, Overcast Grey #D6D9DD, Bluestone #3A3F45, Fluid White #F6F5F2. The real palette is listed under What is missing.
No gosNone stated. Applied: no claim beyond SPF 50+ and the invisible finish until cleared; no competitor cue; no beach, because the brief is about every other day; no shaming of skin.
Endline options for the set
ElegantHalfshade. Whatever the sky.Works with every route and every weather.

Violet Beads

Under a grey Collingwood sky a strand of UV beads on a café table has turned deep violet, and the tube stands beside it.

Why it wins. The sky says grey, the beads say UV, and the tube is the answer, readable at a glance on a shelf or a footpath. (Product truth, High key clean)
