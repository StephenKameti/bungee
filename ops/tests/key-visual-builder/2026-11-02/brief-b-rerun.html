The brief, read back

So everyone signs off on the same problem

Single minded proposition

Proper uji in five minutes. (Verbatim.) Swahili working line: Uji kamili kwa dakika tano.

Product heroMawio 1 kg stand up pouch. Sorghum, finger millet and maize blend.
Business problemCategory entry and occasion: parents skip breakfast uji on school days because the traditional cook is too slow. Make Mawio the school morning default.
Insight (proposed)The morning runs out before the uji is ready, so the children leave on an empty stomach and the parent carries that all day. Five minutes is the difference between sending them out hungry and sending them out full.
AudienceParents 25 to 45 in Dar es Salaam and Mombasa, up before dawn, cooking on gas or charcoal, buying flour at the duka. In their words: asubuhi hakuna muda. (In the morning there is no time.)
ChannelsRadio; duka and kiosk point of sale (shelf talkers, danglers, painted shop walls); daladala and matatu backs; roadside billboards; social status and feed.
MandatoriesPouch on every KV. Swahili and English copy. Price visible.
BudgetLow: six routes, the minimum viable kit first, routes that build from one plate or one cheap real shoot; two colour print for danglers and walls.
Price and currencyPrice must show. Tanzania in TSh, Kenya in KSh, one currency per execution. Not supplied, so marked placeholders: TSh X,XXX and KSh XXX.
PaletteProposed, none supplied: Mawio Gold #F2A900, Dawn Indigo #24306B, Uji Cream #EAD9B0, Clay Red #B5482A, Enamel White #F7F4EC. The real palette is listed under What is missing.
No gosNone stated. Applied: no child's face in frame; no nutrition or brain claim; no shaming of parents who skip breakfast; no competitor flour cue; no poverty set dressing.
Endline options for the set
ElegantMawio. Asubuhi njema.Mawio. Good morning. The brand name means sunrise; the line wishes the morning well.
PlainUji kamili. Dakika tano.Proper uji. Five minutes. The proposition in four words.
ShareableUji kabla ya kengele.Uji before the bell. Recommended. Parents will say it to each other.
