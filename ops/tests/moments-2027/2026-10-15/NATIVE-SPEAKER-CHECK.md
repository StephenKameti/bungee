# Moments 2027: native speaker check list

Thu 15 Oct 2026. Every hook that is not plain English, grouped by language, so each checker gets one short list. The almanac already tells buyers that local hooks need a local check; this is our own check before launch.

**Ask each checker three things per line:** Is it correct? Does it sound natural, like something a brand in your market would post? Would anyone find it off or awkward on that day? Ask for a better line if not.

Budget: about 30 minutes per language. A paid checker for Swahili and Kinyarwanda is worth it; the rest a friend can do.

## Two things to decide first (my findings)

1. **Swahili hooks are tagged to all five East African markets.** Swahili leads in Kenya and Tanzania, but not in Uganda (Luganda and English), Rwanda (Kinyarwanda, English, French) or Zambia (English, Bemba, Nyanja). The pack's own rules say this. Thirteen moments below carry a Swahili hook for all five. Proposal: keep the Swahili hook, retag those hooks to Kenya and Tanzania, and add one line to each angle: "In Uganda, Rwanda and Zambia, lead in English or the local language."
2. **Two hooks are Nairobi slang (Sheng), tagged to all five markets:** "Njaanuary imefika" (m005) and "Ocha season imeanza" (m149). Both land well in Kenya and nowhere else. Proposal: retag both to Kenya.

I have not changed the data yet. Both are quick to apply once a Swahili checker agrees.
