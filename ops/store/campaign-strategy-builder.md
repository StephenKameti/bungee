## Product description

### Board meeting. Monday.

Walk in with the whole strategy. Written at senior planner standard.

**What you get**

* A complete campaign strategy in nine sections, in order
* Situation, audience and three insight options for you to choose from
* Campaign platform and creative territories
* Copy suites with local language adaptation built in
* Channel map with budget split and funnel
* Executions, with motion briefs for the hero films
* A one page creative brief when that is all you need

**Who it is for**

* Strategists and planners under deadline
* Agency leads building a pitch
* Marketing managers who need a plan the board signs off

**Install in three steps**

Unzip your download first. Inside you will find the skill zip, a guide and your licence.

1. In Claude, open **Settings**, then **Capabilities**. Turn on **code execution and file creation**.
2. Go to **Customize**, then **Skills**. Tap **+**, choose **Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**, and pick the skill zip.
3. Switch it on. Start a new chat and ask.

Works on any Claude plan, Free included, with code execution and file creation switched on.

## FAQ

**Can I get just a creative brief?**
Yes. Ask for a one page creative brief and that is what you get.

**Do I need a paid Claude plan?**
No. Any plan works, Free included. Just switch on code execution and file creation in Settings first.

**Will it work in my market?**
Yes. Name your city or country in the request and everything goes local: casting, places, language, channels, prices and cultural dates. Want a market on permanent standby? Fill in the market template inside the skill. A finished East Africa module is included as a worked example.
