## FAQ

**What is in 2027 that I should know now?**
A lot. Ramadan and Lent overlap almost entirely from February into March. The biggest football final in the United States lands on Valentine's Day. October is the busiest month of the year. Hanukkah begins on Christmas Eve. Plan for all of it now.

**How sure are the dates?**
Fixed dates are fixed. Lunar dates are marked expected, so you prepare two versions and post after the official announcement. Organiser dates are marked confirm. Always check official sources before booking media.

**Is it only for one region?**
No. It covers eight regions worldwide. East Africa comes as a regional pack, and more packs follow the same format.
