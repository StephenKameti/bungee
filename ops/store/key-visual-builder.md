## FAQ

**What do I give it?**
Anything you have. A brief, a deck, a PDF, a logo, pack shots or a few typed lines. It reads them all.

**Do I need a paid Claude plan?**
No. Any plan works, Free included. Just switch on code execution and file creation in Settings first.

**What if the routes feel safe?**
Tell it. Say "too safe" or "more local" and it reworks the routes rather than starting over.
