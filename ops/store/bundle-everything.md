## Product description

### Brief at six. Pitch at nine.

All six skill packs plus Moments 2027. Plan the whole year, then make the work.

**What you get**

* Key Visual Builder: one brief in, eight campaign routes out
* Retail Display Theatre: display units that turn aisles into theatre
* Designed Deck Builder: slides that look studio made, copy included
* Social Content Calendar Builder: a full month of posts, built by pillar
* Audience and Persona Builder: real people, real places, real channels
* Campaign Strategy Builder: board ready strategy in nine sections
* Moments 2027: 160 dated moments across eight regions, each with the angle, a hook and what to avoid
* Each pack works in any market you name, with an East Africa module included as a worked example

**Who it is for**

* Agencies and teams planning and making a full year of work
* Freelancers who want the complete kit in one go
* Brand teams who want the calendar and the craft together

**Install in three steps**

Unzip your download first. Each pack has its own folder with a skill zip, a guide and your licence.

1. In Claude, open **Settings**, then **Capabilities**. Turn on **code execution and file creation**. Newer layouts call it **Create and edit files**.
2. Go to **Customize**, then **Skills**. Tap **+**, choose **Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**, and pick a skill zip. Repeat for each pack.
3. Switch them on. Start a new chat and ask. Claude picks the right skill for the job.

Moments 2027 needs no install. Open the almanac page in any browser.

Works on any Claude plan, Free included, with code execution and file creation switched on. Moments 2027 needs no Claude account at all.
