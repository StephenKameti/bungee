## Refund policy (proposal for Stephen)

**Proposed rule:** a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the product will not install or open, and we cannot fix it together. Because every file is delivered instantly and can be kept, we do not refund a change of mind once the download has happened.

Paste version for the store and the website:

> **Refunds**
>
> Our products are digital and arrive the moment you pay. If a skill pack will not install, or the almanac will not open, reply to your receipt within 14 days. We will help you get it working. If we cannot, you get a full refund.
>
> We do not refund a change of mind once a product has been downloaded. If you bought the wrong item by mistake and have not downloaded it, tell us within 14 days and we will refund or swap it.
>
> Where the law where you live gives you more rights, those rights apply.

Notes for Stephen:

* Buyers in the EU and UK have a 14 day right to cancel digital purchases unless they agree at checkout that delivery starts at once and they lose that right. Check that the checkout shows and records this consent.
* The payment platform acts as the seller of record for card sales, so its own buyer terms sit alongside ours. Paystack sales in Kenya are sold by you directly, so this policy is the one that applies there.
