## FAQ

**Is this real research?**
No. It is a fast, grounded starting point built from what Claude knows about the market. Check it against your own data before big decisions.

**Do I need a paid Claude plan?**
No. Any plan works, Free included. Just switch on code execution and file creation in Settings first.

**Will it work in my market?**
Yes. Name your city or country in the request and everything goes local: casting, places, language, channels, prices and cultural dates. Want a market on permanent standby? Fill in the market template inside the skill. A finished East Africa module is included as a worked example.
