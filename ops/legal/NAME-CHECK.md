# Name check: Bungee (head start, not legal advice)

Decision 7 is yours, due Fri 30 Oct. This page saves you the first ten minutes. A public web search on Thu 29 Oct 2026 found two live US filings for the word BUNGEE close to what we sell:

| Mark | Classes | Status found | Overlap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BUNGEE, US serial 97645372, filed 24 Oct 2022 | 42, computer and software services | Live, pending, not yet registered | Bungee Studio is a web app, which sits in class 42 |
| BUNGEE, US serial 88961322, filed 11 Jun 2020 | 9 and 35, recruiting software and services | Live, pending, second extension granted | Class 9 covers downloadable software, so the skill packs touch it |

Older Bungee Labs marks also show up; they look like a closed software company, so check them but expect them to be dead.

Sources: https://markinton.com/trademark/bungee-97645372 and https://trademark.justia.com/889/61/bungee-88961322.html

## What only you can do (about 30 minutes)

1. Search BUNGEE in the WIPO Global Brand Database, https://branddb.wipo.int, filtered to classes 9, 41 and 42. Note anything live in Kenya, the US, the UK or the EU.
2. Search the same at KIPI, https://www.kipi.go.ke.
3. Put the two US filings above in front of a trademark lawyer and ask: can we sell creative skill packs and a SaaS app as Bungee worldwide, and should we file in Kenya now?
4. Record the answer in the Decisions table, row 7.

Neither filing above is registered yet, so neither is a stop sign on its own. Holding paid promotion until the lawyer answers, as the roadmap says, is the safe line.
