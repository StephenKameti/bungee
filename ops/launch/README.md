# Launch kit

Everything for launch week and the weeks after. Products launch Fri 27 Nov 2026, with the Black Friday offer as the launch.

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `LAUNCH-PLAN.md` | Launch day hour by hour, one skill film a day, the four day offer with post copy |
| `MESSAGES.md` | The 50 personal messages in four versions, one follow up, replies to common questions |
| `list-of-50.csv` | Your list: who, where, which message, sent, replied, bought |
| `monday-posts/` | 25 Monday moment posts, 9 Nov 2026 to 26 Apr 2027, each eight weeks ahead of its moments |
| `monday_posts.py` | Rebuilds `monday-posts/` from the almanac data and checks for trademarked names and dashes |
| `set_launch.py` | Moves launch week to any Tuesday up to 17 Nov. Not used on this branch: the 27 Nov launch has no separate launch week |

Film captions live in `films/CAPTIONS.md`. Store copy lives in `ops/store/`.

Nothing here is posted or sent by me. You press send.
