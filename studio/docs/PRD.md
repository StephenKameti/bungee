# Product requirements

## The problem
Agency and brand teams everywhere spend days turning a brief into work a client can react to: routes, retail units, decks, calendars, strategy. Generic AI tools produce generic, placeless work that has to be redone, and they ignore local language, casting, markets and the cultural calendar of wherever the work will run.

## The promise
From brief to board ready in one sitting, with work that looks and sounds like home.

## Who it is for
1. Creative directors who need a real spread of routes fast and want to direct, not type.
2. Designers who need prompts, build sheets and previews that respect the brand kit.
3. Strategists who need a board ready strategy with a defensible insight.
4. Social managers who need a month of posts that knows every cultural moment and when to go quiet.

## Product principles (from docs/RESEARCH.md)
1. Show the plan, then the work. 2. Options, not answers. 3. Documents and canvases, not chat bubbles. 4. Human checkpoints at the decisions that matter. 5. Brand memory everywhere. 6. Speed is a feature. 7. Crafted details. 8. Built for here, including low bandwidth. 9. Own your output. 10. Honest about cost.

## Scope for version 1
Shared: auth, workspaces, brand kits, brief intake and read back, projects library, command palette, exports, share links, billing with credits, moments calendar.
Tools: Key Visual, Retail Theatre, Deck Designer, Content Calendar, Campaign Strategy.
Out of scope for version 1: real time multiplayer editing, native mobile apps, video generation, public API.

## Success metrics
Activation: first export within 20 minutes of sign up for 40 percent of new workspaces.
Quality: at least 60 percent of generated key visual routes shortlisted or exported.
Retention: 50 percent of paying workspaces active in week 4.
Revenue: 100 paying workspaces within 90 days of launch.
Cost: AI cost below 30 percent of revenue per workspace.
