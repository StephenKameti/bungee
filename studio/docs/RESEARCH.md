# What award winning apps teach us

Ten principles, where each comes from, and the exact feature it becomes in Bungee Studio.

| # | Principle | Learned from | Becomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Show the plan, then the work | The 2026 Webby winner for best AI product (a coding agent that plans before it acts and shows progress), and the "action plan" pattern in the Shape of AI pattern library | The read back step with a visible plan, the PlanList that ticks off stages, the "Ask me before each step" toggle |
| 2 | Options, not answers | Image generators that return a grid of variations; the 2026 Webby winner for AI creative tools, a filmmaking tool built around generating many takes and arranging them | Route spreads of 6 to 8, three design languages, three insights, variations filmstrips |
| 3 | Documents and canvases, not chat bubbles | Doc and deck tools that generate structured pages; the "inline action" pattern | Outputs render on paper sheets and canvases; chat is only a small "Direct this" input beside the work |
| 4 | Human checkpoints at real decisions | The human in the loop patterns in the Shape of AI library | Insight and territory choices in strategy, design language choice in decks, route shortlists |
| 5 | Brand memory everywhere | Style presets and voice and tone patterns; Stitch's portable DESIGN.md | Brand kits that feed every tool automatically |
| 6 | Speed is a feature | Keyboard first productivity apps and launcher style command palettes | Cmd K palette, shortcuts, optimistic UI, streaming, background jobs |
| 7 | Crafted details | The 2026 Apple Design Award winner for Delight and Fun: one simple idea, delivered with obsessive craft | Serif route numerals, the horizon line, plain micro copy, the copy button that says "Copied" |
| 8 | Built for here | The 2026 Apple Design Award inclusivity winner from India, designed for diverse abilities | Mobile review flow, low bandwidth thumbnails, local language tabs, sensitive date protection |
| 9 | Own your output | Portable design files that move between tools | Clean exports in every format, share links, no lock in |
| 10 | Honest about cost | The draft mode pattern: explore cheaply, spend compute only on the final | Text is unlimited within fair use, previews cost credits, every button shows its cost first |

## What the 2026 web awards add
Awwwards jurors score design, usability, creativity and content. Their 2026 winners share three things at once: a strong, specific art direction where every type and colour choice serves one idea; directed, meaningful motion that never calls attention to itself; and performance on real mid range devices, because a beautiful page that stutters loses. In 2026 immersive 3D dominated the winners, but for a working tool the lesson is restraint plus craft, not spectacle: one point of view (Darkroom and Lightbox), motion that explains (streaming, plan ticks, page rises), and a hard performance budget.

Sources: Webby Awards 2026 winners (webbyawards.com), Apple Design Awards 2026 (developer.apple.com/design/awards), Awwwards Sites of the Day 2026 (awwwards.com), the Shape of AI pattern library (shapeof.ai).

## Patterns to borrow, screen by screen
- Wayfinders on empty states: show three example briefs a user can click to try.
- Streaming with a caret and a plan list, never a spinner alone.
- Compare mode for two to four options side by side.
- Version history on every output, one click back.
- Inpaint style "Edit area" on image previews.
- Presets for register (Safe, Strong, Wild) and tone (Playful to Premium).

## Anti patterns to avoid
- A chat window as the main interface.
- Sparkle icons and "magic" language. Say what the tool does.
- Spinners with no information for more than two seconds.
- Hidden costs discovered after the click.
- Generic stock people. Every image shows real places in the five markets.
