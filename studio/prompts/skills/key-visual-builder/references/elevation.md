# Elevation: Laws, Mechanisms and the Kill List

The difference between a picture and a Key Visual is a mechanism. This file is the library.

---

## 1. The laws

A law is a borrowed visual rule that makes an ordinary subject extraordinary. Harvest them from references, from other categories, from art. Never imitate a reference's surface. Take its law.

| Law | What it does | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| **Impossible perspective** | Put the camera where a camera cannot go. Inside the cup. Under the pour. Behind the label. Between the crumb | The angle is the idea |
| **Frozen physics** | Stop the moment no eye can hold. Crown splash, suspended droplets, the exact instant of a snap or a shatter | Craft, precision, quality |
| **Surreal isolation** | One object alone in a void. Scale removed. The pack as a monument. The green as an island | Premium, cut through, confidence |
| **Language as terrain** | The line built as a physical environment. Mown into grass, poured in sauce, laid as road, stacked in packs | Ownership. The brand made the world |
| **Absurdist seriousness** | One unexpected object placed with total gravity and zero wink, the logic of high fashion campaigns that photograph an everyday object as a monument | Wit, modernity, shareability |
| **Scale inversion** | Make the small enormous or the enormous small. A single grain the size of a boulder. A whole kitchen inside a sachet | Wonder |
| **Cross section** | Cut the thing open. The layers are the argument | Proof, education, efficacy |
| **The clarity device** | A lens, ring, halo, grid, arrow of light placed inside the frame that tells the eye where to look | Demonstration. Essential for hygiene and health |
| **Hard split** | The frame divided down the centre. Two worlds, two colours, one subject | Before and after, with and without, the argument in one image |
| **Colour as verdict** | One half the frame drained and grey, the other saturated in brand colour. The palette does the persuading | Instant comprehension at the fixture |
| **The empty chair** | Show the absence rather than the presence. What is missing is the point | Emotional weight, behaviour change |
| **Documentary intrusion** | One utterly real detail in an otherwise composed frame. A worn handle, a chipped tile, a real market receipt | Truth. Kills the stock feeling |

## 2. How to harvest a law from a reference

When the user supplies a reference wall, a global KV, a competitor ad, or a screenshot they liked, always present this table before the prompts.

| Reference | What it said | What I did with it |
|---|---|---|
| A global deodorant category split screen KV | The argument lives in the divide, not in the copy | Kept the hard split, moved the clarity devices inside each underarm so the eye has somewhere to land |
| Two ink linocut wall | Craft reads through restraint, not detail | Two colour illustration on the wrap, brand orange and deep brown only |
| An oversized luxury fashion accessory campaign | Total seriousness applied to an absurd scale | The 10kg pack shot as a monument on a stainless bench |

**Never** write "inspired by the reference." State the law.

## 3. Mechanisms by proposition type

When stuck, match the proposition to a mechanism.

| The proposition is | Reach for |
|---|---|
| Our product works better | Hard split, colour as verdict, clarity device, cross section |
| Our product is made with more care | Cross section, frozen physics, impossible perspective, documentary intrusion |
| Buy more of it | Scale inversion, language as terrain built from the packs, the tableau that is visibly incomplete |
| It belongs at this occasion | Occasion tableau with one documentary intrusion, the empty chair |
| We are the real one | Surreal isolation, heritage detail shot as evidence, the cut face |
| Change what you do | The empty chair, hard split across time not space, clarity device on the consequence |
| We are new here | Absurdist seriousness, surreal isolation, the mirror |
| It is worth the price | Editorial luxury register plus cross section. Show what is inside the premium |

## 4. The kill list

Delete these on sight. If a brief demands one, argue once, then execute it better than anyone has.

- Smiling family around a table
- Hands presenting a product toward camera
- Generic golden hour sunset behind a silhouette
- Ingredients exploding in midair with no reason for the explosion
- A woman laughing alone with food
- A gradient background with a floating pack and a starburst
- A single iconic natural landmark or silhouette standing in for an entire region or continent, for example a baobab tree, an acacia silhouette or a savannah sunset standing in for all of Africa, or an equivalent lazy shorthand for any other region
- Anyone pointing at anything
- A person holding a phone showing an app screen
- Confetti, unless the brief is literally a celebration
- A handshake
- A lightbulb
- Puzzle pieces
- Anything described as "vibrant and dynamic"

---

## 5. The three questions before a route ships

1. Cover the headline. Does the picture still mean something?
2. Crop it to a 2:1 billboard. Does one idea survive?
3. Could a competitor in the same category run this exact image tomorrow? If yes, it is a category visual, not a brand KV. Kill it.
