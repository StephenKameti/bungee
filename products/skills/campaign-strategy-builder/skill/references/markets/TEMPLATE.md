## Audience and society

How this market's audience architecture actually works. Cover: neighbourhood, city or regional context that carries real class and character signals (name real places, not a generic label), whether household and category decisions skew toward one tier by default and how confident that assumption is, social structures worth building into the Tier 2 and Tier 3 model (community networks, savings or trade groups, religious or civic institutions), and the cultural moment worth naming in a situation analysis for the live period.

## Language and copy rules

The working language of business and advertising in this market, and the local language or register the copy suite's local line should carry. Cover: how the two blend naturally in real speech, which register (formal, youth slang, regional dialect) fits which audience tier, at least two worked example lines with an English gloss, a note on tone rules specific to this market (openings to avoid, registers that read as condescending), and any other market that shares this market's dominant language but should not have lines reused unchanged.

## Media and channels

The standard channel set for this market, described generically by channel type rather than by naming any platform, broadcaster, retailer or outlet: broadcast reach, radio's role and language split, how out-of-home is actually viewed and where the routes with real reach are, social and short-video behaviour by audience tier, messaging-app or group-sharing behaviour, and activation formats (retail, community, institutional) worth building into a channel map.

## Retail and price

The retail formats and price mechanics that shape a shopper campaign in this market: modern trade described generically, general or informal trade if it is a meaningful channel, dominant payment behaviour at the till, and small-pack or price-point mechanics worth reflecting in creative territories and executions.

## Casting and settings

How this market's talent, locations and production register should be described by context, not by stacked category words. Cover: everyday casting register versus stock or editorial register, real named locations worth using instead of "urban setting," and dress code or occasion signals that change with class or setting.

## Cultural moments pointer
