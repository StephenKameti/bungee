# Campaign Strategy Builder by Bungee

Board ready campaign strategy documents for any market: situation, audience, insight, platform, creative territories, copy suites with local language adaptation, channel map and executions with motion briefs for the hero films, written at senior planner standard.

## What is in the box
* `campaign-strategy-builder.zip`: the Claude skill, ready to upload
* `README.md`: this guide
* `LICENSE.txt`: your licence

## Install (2 minutes)
1. Open Claude and go to your Skills settings (skills need a paid plan with code execution on).
2. Upload `campaign-strategy-builder.zip`.
3. Start a new chat. The skill switches on by itself when your request matches.

## Try it with
* Write the motion brief for the 30 second hero film
* Build a full campaign strategy for a new flour brand in Indonesia
* Write the insight and three creative territories for this brief
* Turn this research deck into a creative brief

## Markets
Name the market in your request and the skill builds everything local from it: casting, places, language, channels, prices and cultural dates. To make a market permanent, copy `references/markets/TEMPLATE.md` inside the skill, fill it in, and save it as `references/markets/<your-market>.md`. The East Africa module is included as a worked example.

## Getting the best results
* Attach whatever you have: brief, deck, logo, pack shots, reference images. The skill reads them.
* Push back. Say "too safe", "too corporate" or "more local" and it reworks rather than restarts.
* Image prompts are tuned for the leading image models and adapt to others with light edits.
