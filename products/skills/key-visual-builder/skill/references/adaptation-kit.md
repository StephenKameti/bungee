# Adaptation Kit

The master KV is one plate. The campaign is thirty. This file is how one becomes thirty without losing the idea.

---

## 1. Crop or reprompt

The single most useful call in the whole kit. Get it wrong and the artworker loses a day.

| Situation | Call |
|---|---|
| Same orientation, tighter | **Crop.** 4:5 master to 1:1 feed |
| Same orientation, wider | **Reprompt.** You cannot invent frame edges by cropping |
| Orientation flips | **Reprompt.** Always. 4:5 to 16:9 and 4:5 to 9:16 are new compositions |
| Text zone moves to the other side | **Reprompt** |
| Subject changes size relative to frame by more than a third | **Reprompt** |
| Colour or time of day changes | **Image to image pass**, one variable only |

State the call explicitly on every line of every adaptation kit.

## 2. Digital and social

| Format | Ratio | Pixels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Master KV | 4:5 | 2160 x 2700 | Everything derives from here. Build at 300dpi if it also goes to print |
| Feed post | 1:1 | 2000 x 2000 | Tighten to the mechanism. First crop to test |
| Portrait feed | 4:5 | 1080 x 1350 | Usually the master downsized |
| Story and short vertical video | 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 | Reprompt. Text zone upper third, subject lower two thirds, keep the bottom 250px clear of the UI |
| OLV end frame | 16:9 | 1920 x 1080 | Reprompt. Text zone on a clean side third, endline and pack lockup only |
| Video platform thumbnail | 16:9 | 1280 x 720 | One face or one object. Nothing else reads |
| Display banner set | 300x250, 728x90, 160x600, 320x50 | | Reprompt each. The leaderboard and skyscraper are different pictures |
| Chat app forward | 4:5 | 1080 x 1350 | The real distribution channel in many markets, more so than the feed itself. Check the market module for which chat app dominates locally. Test legibility at thumbnail size |

## 3. Out of home

| Format | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Billboard 6x3m | 2:1 | One element only. The mechanism at scale plus the endline on a clean third. Viewed at speed from a moving vehicle |
| Billboard 12x4m | 3:1 | Reprompt wide. Subject on one third, endline and pack on the other |
| Street pole | 1:3 tall | Vertical stack. Pack, mechanism, line. No environment |
| Backlit lightbox | 4:5 or 1:1 | High key clean register only. Dark registers die in a lightbox |
| Street furniture 6 sheet (bus shelter, metro, mall) | about 2:3 portrait, 1185 x 1750 mm visible | Backlit, read on foot at 2 to 5 m. Usually a crop of the 4:5 master; lift the grade one stop for the lightbox |
| Wall wrap or building | Bespoke | Confirm the architectural obstructions before designing |
| Public transport branding | Long horizontal | Assume a third of the artwork is obscured by windows and doors. Build redundancy. Check the market module for the dominant local transport format, minibus, bus, tram, tuk tuk |

Standard sizes vary by market; confirm them with the media owner. 8x3 m is the roadside standard in some European markets; 12x3 m and 40x10 ft are common in parts of Africa and Asia.

**OOH rule:** cover everything except one element. If the ad still communicates, it is an OOH KV. If not, it is a press ad.

## 4. Shopper and point of sale

The format set that most often gets forgotten until the week before print.

| Item | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shelf talker | 3:4 tall narrow | Pack plus one visual cue. Zero environment. Read at 40cm in bad supermarket light |
| Wobbler | 1:1 small | The mechanism only. One colour block |
| Gondola end | 1:2 tall | Build a vertical world. Pack repeated, headline at adult eye height, price plate left clear |
| Dangler | 1:1 double sided | Same mechanism both faces. No small type, it spins |
| Floor vinyl | 1:1 | Read from directly above at walking pace. Text one direction only |
| Bin wrap or dump bin | Wraparound | Design as a dieline. Confirm the panel widths before prompting |
| Danglers and buntings | Small repeat | Brand colour and one icon. Nothing photographic survives |
| Trolley panel | 2:1 | Endline and pack |

**Shopper rule:** comprehension in three to five seconds at the fixture. That is the actual brief. Anything that needs a second look has failed.

## 5. Print and press

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Full page press | Add 3mm bleed. Convert palette to CMYK and flag any hex that will shift. Brand oranges and turquoises shift most |
| Half page horizontal | Reprompt. It is not a cropped full page |
| Flyer and leaflet | Front carries the KV, back carries the detail. Never split the mechanism across a fold |
| Roll up banner 2m | 1:2.5. Bottom 400mm is below eye level and gets blocked by a table. Keep it clear |

---

## 6. Video derivatives

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| TVC end frame | The KV, still, plus endline and pack lockup. Should be the master at 16:9. Brief it as a 2 bar end frame |
| Bumper 6s | The mechanism resolving in one move. Storyboard as three frames from the same base prompt, then write the 3 bar motion brief |
| Social cutdown covers | 9:16 vertical reprompts, one per cutdown |
| Animated KV | From the approved master. One movement only: steam rising, light shifting, a slow push in. Photographic movement goes to an image to video model; type, pack slams and the logo landing are code drawn on top |
| Short vertical video | 10 to 15 s built from the route: hook in 2 s, two beats of value, call to action. Restack for 9:16, never crop a horizontal master |

Every moving item gets a motion brief: see `references/motion-brief.md` for the anatomy, the beat grid shot list, the readability law and the format presets.

---

## 7. Audio

Radio is not a crop or a reprompt. Per route, write a 15 s script in the market's spoken language: the mechanism as a sound effect, the line said twice, the endline, the price said once, about 2.5 words a second. Note the station language; regional and vernacular stations often carry the reach.

## 8. Activation and sampling

| Item | Ratio | Call | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gazebo or canopy valance | Long strip | **Designer build** | Two colours, the endline |
| Stall A board | 2:3 | **Crop** | Read from the walkway at 2 to 4 m |
| Demo backdrop or roll up | 1:2.5 | **Reprompt** | The bottom 400mm is hidden by the table. Keep it clear |
| Promoter apron or shirt | Garment | **Designer build** | The endline and one icon, screen printed |
| Bunting | Small repeat | **Designer build** | One icon, nothing photographic |
| Take home sampler card | Card | **Designer build** | Front carries the mechanism, back carries the price and the CTA |

---

## 9. The minimum viable kit

When time is short, these five cover 90 percent of a shopper campaign launch in most markets.

1. Master 4:5
2. Feed 1:1
3. Story 9:16
4. Billboard 2:1
5. Shelf talker 3:4

Deliver these five first, name them as the priority set, and build the rest on approval.
