# Global Cultural Moments: Base Dataset

## Purpose

This is the starting layer for the cultural moment calendar. It lists moments that recur across many markets in some form. It is never sufficient on its own, load the market module (`references/markets/<market>.md`) for that market's own fixed dates, monthly rhythms, sporting anchors and hashtag sets, and layer the two together. Where the two disagree on framing or weight, the market module wins, since exact dates and local significance vary by market.

## How to use this file

1. Identify the market or markets in the brief (see the market layer step in SKILL.md).
2. Pull the moments below that apply to those markets, confirming exact dates locally, some of these moments move by market, calendar system, or year.
3. Add the market's own moments from its market module.
4. Flag every sensitive moment (see below) before scheduling any promotional content.

## Recurring global moments (confirm local date and relevance per market)

```
JAN 1        New year, aspiration and fresh start content, near universal
FEB 14       A romantic or gifting occasion in many markets, confirm the
             local equivalent and adoption level before leaning on it
MAR 8        International Women's Day, substantive content only, avoid
             shallow "we celebrate women" posts
Spring       A major spring holiday period, dates and religious basis vary
             by market (Easter, Passover, Nowruz, Songkran, and others),
             family, renewal, travel
Labour       A worker's or labour day, date varies by market (commonly
             May 1 or early September), worker appreciation content
Mother's Day A mother's day or equivalent, date varies widely by market
             (commonly a moveable Sunday or a fixed date), one of the
             highest-engagement days of the year for family, food and
             personal care brands where observed
Father's Day A father's day or equivalent, smaller than mother's day in
             most markets but present in many
National Day A major national or independence day, always market-specific,
             never generic flag imagery
Year-end     A major end-of-year faith or harvest festival relevant to
             the market (a winter holiday, a harvest festival, or a major
             faith calendar date), confirm which applies locally
Shopping     A year-end discount shopping event (commonly in November),
             value and deals content, audiences are sceptical of inflated
             discounts
Festive      The year-end holiday season and a new year's eve moment,
             the most emotional content window of the year in most
             markets, reward consistency rather than a first appearance
```

## Moveable faith and lunar dates (check every month)

These move against the solar calendar every year, so a fixed list misses them. Before building any month, check whether one of these falls in it or in the week before, confirm the exact local date for the year, and flag any date set by moon sighting as provisional.

```
Ramadan      A month of daytime fasting, about 11 days earlier each
             year, start and end set by moon sighting. Shapes the whole
             month where Muslims are many: post food near the evening
             meal and after it, shift posting times, keep content
             welcoming to those not fasting
Eid al Fitr  The feast that ends Ramadan, one to three days, often a
             public holiday; date confirmed only the evening before
Eid al Adha  About 70 days after Eid al Fitr; family, sharing, food
Lunar new    Late January to mid February in markets on the Chinese,
year         Korean or Vietnamese lunar calendar; often a multi day
             holiday with travel home and gifting
Diwali       October or November; lights, sweets, gifting, family
Holi         February or March; colour, play, spring
Vesak        April to June; quiet and reflective in Buddhist markets
Passover, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur   Spring and early autumn; Yom
             Kippur is a fast, treat it as a quiet day
Easter       Western and Orthodox dates differ most years; Good Friday
             is a quiet day in many Christian markets
```

## Sensitive moments (always a rule, not a suggestion)

No promotional content on remembrance days, disaster anniversaries, election dates, or protest or uprising anniversaries specific to the market. These dates are not listed here because they are market-specific, find them in the market module, in the brief, or through direct research before finalising any calendar. When a sensitive date falls inside the target month and no module covers it, ask rather than guess.

## What this file will not tell you

Exact dates for moveable or market-specific observances, local monthly spending and social rhythms, sporting anchors, hashtag sets, and language or caption rules. All of that lives in the market module. This file only establishes that the mechanism, fixed dates, moveable dates, monthly rhythm, sensitive dates, repeats everywhere.
