# Social Content Calendar Builder by Bungee

Complete monthly social calendars for any brand in any market: pillars, platform specific posts, captions in the market's languages, a compact motion brief for every short video post, and a cultural moments layer that tracks moveable feasts and knows when to go quiet. Prices and claims come from your brief, never invented. Ships with the East Africa market module; add your own from the template.

## What is in the box
* `social-content-calendar-builder.zip`: the Claude skill, ready to upload
* `README.md`: this guide
* `LICENSE.txt`: your licence

## Install (2 minutes)
1. Open Claude and go to your Skills settings (skills work on every Claude plan, Free included, with code execution and file creation on; newer layouts call it Create and edit files).
2. Upload `social-content-calendar-builder.zip`.
3. Start a new chat. The skill switches on by itself when your request matches.

## Try it with
* Give me five short video posts for launch week, with motion briefs
* Build me an October content calendar for a coffee brand in Lisbon
* What should a bank post for Diwali in Mumbai?
* Give me short video and photo feed captions for our back to school push

## Markets
Name the market in your request and the skill builds everything local from it: casting, places, language, channels, prices and cultural dates. To make a market permanent, copy `references/markets/TEMPLATE.md` inside the skill, fill it in, and save it as `references/markets/<your-market>.md`. The East Africa module is included as a worked example.

## Getting the best results
* Attach whatever you have: brief, deck, logo, pack shots, reference images. The skill reads them.
* Push back. Say "too safe", "too corporate" or "more local" and it reworks rather than restarts.
* Image prompts are tuned for the leading image models and adapt to others with light edits.
