# Moments 2027 by Bungee

A planner's almanac of 160 dated moments: 88 global holidays, faiths, festivals, sport and shopping seasons across eight regions, plus the East Africa regional pack. Each moment carries the content angle, a hook to copy and what to avoid.

## What is in the box
* `Moments-2027.html`: the interactive almanac. Filter by region and type, search, star your picks and export them as a calendar file. Works offline in any browser.
* `Moments-2027.ics`: import every moment into any calendar app.
* `Moments-2027.csv`: open in any spreadsheet to build your own plan.
* `moments-2027.json`: the raw data for your own tools.

## The stories of 2027
* Ramadan and Lent overlap almost entirely from February into March.
* The biggest advertising night in the United States lands on Valentine's Day.
* Brazil hosts the first Women's World Cup in South America, 24 June to 25 July.
* October is the busiest month: the Jewish High Holidays, Diwali, two World Cups, Golden Week and Halloween.
* Hanukkah begins on 24 December and overlaps Christmas.

## Reading the dates
* Expected: lunar dates that depend on sighting or local calendars. Prepare two versions and post after the official announcement in each market.
* Confirm: set by organisers each year.
* Sensitive: remembrance days, elections and anniversaries where brands should go quiet in that market.
* Governments add and move holidays by decree. Check official notices before booking media.

Local language hooks are starting points. Have a native speaker check every line before it runs. Tournament and league names are used descriptively; using them in advertising needs rights.

## Regional packs
East Africa (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Zambia) is included. More packs follow the same format; the JSON schema is the template.
