Types
Moon and lunar dates

Islamic, Hindu, Buddhist and East Asian dates follow lunar calendars and are shown on expected days. Keep two versions ready and post after the official announcement in each market.

Moving dates

Items marked Confirm are set by organisers each year. Governments add and move holidays, so check official notices before booking media.

Go quiet dates

Remembrance days, elections and anniversaries of tragedy are marked red. No promotional content in that market on those dates. Regional packs add local ones.
