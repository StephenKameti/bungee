# Bungee brand for motion

## Logo
- `brand/bungee-logo-transparent.png` (1852 x 933, transparent background): the master for compositing.
- `brand/bungee-logo-trim.png` (1318 x 411, trimmed to the mark): what the films place.
- `brand/bungee-logo-whitebg.png` (1126 x 482): white background version, for reference only.
- No SVG supplied. The PNG is sharp up to about 1300 px wide, which covers every lockup size in these films.
- Never recolour, stretch or redraw. The mark only sits on light grounds. On slate scenes, the film cuts to paper before the logo lands.
- The lime tick on the g is cut from the real PNG as its own layer (`brand/tick.png`) so it can land separately. Same pixels, never redrawn.

## Colour (sampled from the logo file)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Slate | #3F414B | logo ink, headlines, dark scene ground |
| Lime | #BEFD50 | the tick, the cord, highlight fills, active states. Never as text on light ground |
| Paper | #F6F6F2 | default canvas |
| Hairline | #DCDDD6 | rules and card borders on paper |
| Muted | #7E8089 | secondary labels on paper |
| White | #FFFFFF | cards on paper, text on slate |
Contrast: slate on paper about 10:1, slate on lime about 9:1, lime on slate about 9:1, white on slate about 10:1.

## Type
- Display: Instrument Serif (regular and italic). High contrast and sharp, it sits next to the logo's cut serifs without competing.
- Interface and labels: Hanken Grotesk 500 and 600. Never all caps.
- Prompts and code: JetBrains Mono.
- These match the Bungee Studio design system, so the films and the app read as one brand.
- Exception: the Moments 2027 film uses the almanac's own faces (Fraunces and Manrope) and category colours, because it shows the real product. Only its lockup is in the Bungee system.

## Signature device: the cord
A lime line drops from the top edge, stretches past its rest point and recoils once, like a bungee cord. It pulls cards into frame and yanks finished ideas out. The cord and the logo landing are the only places a visible overshoot is allowed. Product UI moves on smooth springs with no bounce.

## Voice
Short, confident, warm. Problem first, then the fix. No hyphens or em dashes on screen. No brand names other than Bungee.
