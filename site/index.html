How it works

Three steps. Then just ask.

Unzip your download first. Each pack has a skill zip, a short guide and your licence.

Switch on file creation

In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities. Turn on code execution and file creation. Newer layouts call it Create and edit files.

Upload the skill

Go to Customize, then Skills. Tap +, choose Create skill, then Upload a skill, and pick the skill zip.

Brief it

Switch it on, start a new chat and ask. Claude picks the right skill for the job.

Questions

Good questions.

Do I need a paid Claude plan?

No. Any plan works, Free included. Just switch on code execution and file creation in Settings first. Moments 2027 needs no Claude account at all.

What do I give it?

Anything you have. A brief, a deck, a PDF, a logo or a few typed lines. If something is missing, the packs carry on with clear stated assumptions and tell you what to check.

Does it work outside East Africa?

Yes. Every pack is built for any market you name. East Africa is included as a worked regional module, never the default.

Can my team use it?

Each purchase is a single user commercial licence: use it for your own client and personal work, paid work included. Team and agency licences are available. Reply to your receipt.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Everything you make with the packs is yours.

Can I pay with mobile money?

Yes. Card payments run worldwide, and mobile money and local cards are available in Kenya.
